THE PRINCIPLE

Every intervention is a change to a coupled system. Giving one approach more green pushes its platoon into the next junction. Closing a link is not removing traffic — it is relocating it. Watch the map, not the dashboard: congestion that appears three junctions away is usually your own doing.

SYSTEM INSIGHT

Complex systems are interconnected. An intervention designed to improve one component can alter the behaviour of other components, creating secondary effects. Effective engineering therefore requires evaluating system-wide consequences rather than optimising a single variable.
